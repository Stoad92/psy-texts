Overall Psychological Portrait

LWEF ("Scientist") is an independent thinker with a gentle personality and a sensitive heart.

They have powerful logical thinking and firm beliefs, but they never force their opinions on other people. Instead, they prefer respectful conversations between equals.

On the outside they may seem cold, distant, and completely uninterested in comfort or everyday life.

Behind that calm mask, however, is a sensitive person who experiences emotions deeply inside and strongly needs honest warmth and acceptance.
